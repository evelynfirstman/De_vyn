## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the clinical expertise of modern physical therapy combined with the calm, restorative luxury of high-end wellness. It speaks to desk-bound knowledge workers and corporate professionals experiencing chronic postural strain, ergonomic fatigue, and repetitive stress. 

The aesthetic style is **Corporate / Modern infused with Tactile Wellness Minimalism**. The interface prioritizes mental decompression and clinical trust through deep botanical greens, disciplined baseline grids, breathable whitespace, and precision data visualizations. The overall atmosphere rejects the cold, sterile clinicalism of traditional hospital portals in favor of an inviting, high-touch ritual space—resembling an ergonomic sanctuary that instills restorative agency, focused momentum, and medical-grade assurance.

## Colors

The palette establishes clinical authority and organic restoration through deliberate hierarchy:

- **Primary Brand Tier (Deep Restorative Forest)**:
  - Hero Deep Forest: `#0B3D36` (Headings, primary hero modules, brand anchor points)
  - Primary Base: `#12836F` (Primary interactive states, active indicators, CTAs)
  - Primary Hover: `#0E5F54` (Interactive hover and pressed states)
  - Primary Subtle Surface: `#DCF2EA` (Selected card backgrounds, subtle highlight fills)
  - Primary Ghost: `#EEFAF5` (Row striping, hover containers, background badges)

- **Energy & Kinetic Streak Tier**:
  - Kinetic Orange: `#F97316` (Streak counters, vital heatmaps, high-priority notifications)
  - Kinetic Deep Orange: `#D9622B` (Hover states for accent items)
  - Kinetic Glow: `#FFEDD5` (Background pills for active rehabilitation streaks)

- **Semantic Recovery Score Bands**:
  - Rest Required (`Score < 50`): `#E5484D`
  - Easy Day / Maintenance (`Score 50-79`): `#F5A524`
  - Ready / Optimal Function (`Score 80-100`): `#18A957`

- **Crisp Neutrals & Foundation**:
  - Ink 900: `#101828` (Primary typography and metrics)
  - Ink 700: `#344054` (Secondary text, field labels, metadata)
  - Ink 500: `#667085` (Tertiary descriptions, subtle timestamps, placeholders)
  - Border Subdued: `#F2F4F7` (Primary dividers, card borders, table gutters)
  - Surface Background: `#F9FAFB` (Application canvas)
  - Pure White: `#FFFFFF` (Surface cards, modal dialogs, elevated modules)

## Typography

The design system relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, a refined humanist sans-serif that synthesizes geometric precision with organic warmth. Its clear apertures, balanced x-height, and sculpted letter terminals ensure rapid scan-ability of clinical instructions and recovery metrics while remaining warm and non-intimidating.

- **Display & Headlines**: Tightly tracked (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor clinical confidence and editorial structure.
- **Body Text**: Tuned for high legibility during prolonged screen sessions, maintaining generous line-height ratios (1.5x - 1.6x) to reduce optical fatigue.
- **Labels & Overlines**: Use slightly tracked uppercase styling (`label-caps`) for anatomical categorizations (e.g., "CERVICAL SPINE", "THORACIC MOBILITY") and sensor telemetry readouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial architecture is calibrated on an exact **4pt/8pt rhythm**, ensuring uniform vertical cadence and balanced visual density across clinical metrics and exercise media.

- **Layout Structure**: 
  - **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12-column responsive grid with a max container width of 1360px, `2rem` gutters, and `3rem` screen margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8-column responsive grid, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` margins.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` edge margins. Primary daily routine actions stay pinned in a safe-area-compliant bottom action sheet.

- **Component Spacing Principle**: 
  - Tightly grouped data (e.g., metric values paired with units) uses `space-xs` (4px).
  - Standard card interior padding scales from `space-md` (16px) on mobile viewports to `space-lg` (24px) on desktop viewports.
  - Structural separation between independent recovery modules utilizes `space-xl` (40px).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated with architectural restraint through **Tonal Layers and Whisper-Soft Ambient Shadows**. The design avoids stark drop-shadows, leaning instead into subtle luminance steps and natural light diffusion:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: The foundational application layer sits at `#F9FAFB`.
- **Level 1 (Clinical Cards & Interactive Tiles)**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surfaces paired with a razor-thin border of `1px solid #F2F4F7` and a soft ambient glow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.05), 0px 1px 2px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Floating Toolbars, Active Drill Overlays)**: `#FFFFFF` surfaces with `1px solid #F2F4F7`, elevated with `0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(11, 61, 54, 0.06), 0px 4px 6px -2px rgba(11, 61, 54, 0.03)`. The green undertone anchors the shadow naturally to the primary brand pigment.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets, Exercise Fullscreens)**: Elevated using `0px 20px 24px -4px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.08), 0px 8px 8px -4px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.03)` with a semi-opaque backdrop blur (`rgba(11, 61, 54, 0.4)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Level 2 Roundedness** profile. 

- Interactive elements such as buttons, text inputs, and chips utilize a base border-radius of `0.5rem` (8px), striking a balanced equilibrium between ergonomic warmth and medical authority.
- Mid-sized structural cards and diagnostic panels employ `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px).
- Modals, prominent hero modules, and recovery telemetry pods utilize `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px).
- Streak pills, bio-metric status indicators, and micro-badges are set to fully rounded stadium shapes (`9999px`) to visually signal self-contained physical health state trackers.

## Components

- **Buttons**:
  - *Primary*: Solid `#12836F` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0.5rem` border-radius, font `label-lg`. Hover transforms to `#0E5F54` with a smooth 150ms ease-out transition. Active click downscales to `0.98`.
  - *Secondary / Outline*: Pure `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #F2F4F7`, `#101828` text. Hover shifts background to `#EEFAF5` and border to `#12836F`.
  - *Kinetic / Accent*: `#F97316` background with `#FFFFFF` text, used exclusively for active routine triggers, session completion, and streak renewals.

- **Chips & Badges**:
  - *Recovery Score Chip*: Stadium-shaped (`9999px`), padding `4px 12px`. Displays background tint paired with a high-contrast dot (e.g., `#EEFAF5` background with `#18A957` indicator dot and text for "Ready").
  - *Kinetic Streak Badge*: Background `#FFEDD5`, text `#D9622B`, containing an embedded flame or energy glyph, tracking continuous daily rehabilitation habits.

- **Lists & Data Rows**:
  - Protocol checklists feature `1px solid #F2F4F7` horizontal separator lines. Hover states shift the full row background to `#EEFAF5` with transition smoothing. Right-aligned affordances display time elapsed, target muscle grouping, or difficulty level in `body-sm` (`#667085`).

- **Checkboxes & Radio Selectors**:
  - Custom geometric inputs with `4px` radius for checkboxes, full circle for radios. Unchecked: `1.5px solid #667085` on `#FFFFFF`. Checked: solid `#12836F` with a crisp white tick mark. Focus ring delivers a `3px` diffusion in `#DCF2EA`.

- **Input Fields**:
  - Container heights standard at `44px` for touch accuracy. Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #F2F4F7`, text `body-md` in `#101828`. Active focus triggers a border shift to `#12836F` with a matching `3px` focus ring glow in `rgba(18, 131, 111, 0.15)`.

- **Cards**:
  - *Metric Pods*: `#FFFFFF` surface, `1rem` radius, `1px solid #F2F4F7`. Features an asymmetric micro-indicator strip on the top edge tinted to the user's specific recovery tier (Green, Amber, or Red).
  - *Prescribed Routine Cards*: High-contrast hero layout with `#0B3D36` deep forest canvas, `#FFFFFF` typography, embedded countdown timers, and dynamic SVG posture tracking silhouettes.

- **Specialized Product Components**:
  - *Spine & Ergonomic Load Heatmap*: SVG-based anatomical silhouette utilizing tinted target points that shift dynamically between Semantic Neutral (`#F2F4F7`), Rest (`#E5484D`), and Ready (`#18A957`) based on AI posture sensing inputs.
  - *Micro-Break Drawer*: Fixed subtle bottom banner floating at `Level 2` elevation that gently surfaces desk reset cues (e.g., "30-second Cervical Retraction") during periods of prolonged static posture.
